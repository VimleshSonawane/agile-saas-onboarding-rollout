Agile Sprint Board: SaaS Onboarding Rollout (Simulated)

Simulated project by Vimlesh Sonawane, Project Manager. Not a production system, no real customer data. Timeline in the project: 6 weeks, 3 two-week sprints. Target in the simulation: onboarding from 21 days to 10 days — a target, not a real company result. Story points here are for demo illustration, not a real team's velocity.

At a glance

Simulated

Scenario: A simulated B2B partner/client onboarding rollout. In the scenario, the manual process took 21 days and depended on scattered checklists, email follow-ups, and a few individuals. Plan: 6 weeks, 3 two-week sprints, 10 user stories (48 illustrative points) across 5 epics, with a charter, 4 risks with mitigations, and a retrospective. Target: cut onboarding to 10 days (a target in the simulation, not a real company result). Try it: move stories across the board, watch remaining work change, add a backlog story, and click a risk to see its mitigation. What it shows: backlog refinement, sprint planning with a goal and a point total, risk and dependency tracking, and the trade-off when a story isn't finished. Role: Project manager (simulated).

How success is judged (from the charter)

  1. A customer can see every onboarding stage, owner, and next step in one view
  2. Training handoff has a written completion checklist, not an informal handover
  3. Security review is completed before pilot go-live, not after
  4. Pilot feedback is collected, analysed, and used to change at least one flow step before full rollout
  5. A retrospective records what worked, what did not, and what to change next time

Risks and blockers — from docs/raid-log.md

What this shows: backlog refinement, sprint planning with a goal and a point total, a Jira-style board, tracking by remaining work, risk and dependency management, and the trade-off when a story is not finished — it stays out of Done and out of the sprint's completed points.
