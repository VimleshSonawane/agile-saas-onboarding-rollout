Agile Board Demo — SaaS Onboarding Rollout

Simulated project by Vimlesh Sonawane, Project Manager. Not a production system, no real customer data. Timeline in the project: 6 weeks, 3 two-week sprints. Target in the simulation: onboarding from 21 days to 10 days — a target, not a real company result. Story points here are for demo illustration, not a real team's velocity.

How to try it

  1. Pick a sprint below. Read its goal.
  2. Click Move forward on a story card to move it To Do → In Progress → In Review → Done.
  3. Watch sprint progress and remaining work fall as stories reach Done.
  4. Use Reset sprint to run it again. The Backlog tab shows every story, its epic, points, acceptance criteria, and planned sprint.

Remaining work

Points left as stories move to Done. Start = sprint total. Each Done story drops the line.

Product Backlog

From docs/backlog.md. Stories are small enough for one sprint, with acceptance criteria. Future backlog, not in Sprint 1–3: additional import formats, automated reminders, deeper reporting.

What this shows: backlog refinement, sprint planning with a goal and a point total, a Jira-style board, tracking by remaining work, and the trade-off when a story is not finished — it stays out of Done and out of the sprint's completed points. Sprint review and retrospective notes are in docs/sprint-plans.md and docs/retrospective.md.
