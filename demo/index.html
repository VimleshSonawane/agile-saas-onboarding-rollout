Agile Sprint Board: SaaS Onboarding Rollout (Simulated)

Simulated project by Vimlesh Sonawane, Project Manager. Not a production system, no real customer data. Timeline in the project: 6 weeks, 3 two-week sprints. Target in the simulation: onboarding from 21 days to 10 days — a target, not a real company result. Story points here are for demo illustration, not a real team's velocity.

1. Sprint board — try it

Pick a sprint, read its goal, click Move forward on a story to move it To Do → In Progress → In Review → Done. Points and remaining work change only when a story reaches Done. Use Reset sprint to run it again.

Remaining points as stories reach Done

Points left as stories move to Done. Start = sprint total. Each Done story drops the line.

One bar per story, in list order. Each bar shows the points still remaining after that story is done.

Product Backlog

From docs/backlog.md, plus any stories you add below. Future backlog, not in Sprint 1–3: additional import formats, automated reminders, deeper reporting.

Add your own story

Try backlog refinement: add a story for a stakeholder, reporting, or process need. It is added to the backlog in this browser session only, it is not saved to the repo.
