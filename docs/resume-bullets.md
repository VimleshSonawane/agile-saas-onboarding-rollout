# Resume Bullets — Honest Language Draft

Use these only with the label "Project" on the resume/portfolio. They describe what was created and analysed in this self-initiated project, not work for an employer. Do not upgrade "coordinated" to "led", do not add employers, team sizes, budgets, or results that are not in this repo, and do not present simulation targets as real company results.

## Bullets in the agreed language

- Created a self-initiated project for a partner/client onboarding rollout, including charter, backlog, sprint plans, RAID log, and a working demo.
- Analysed a manual, 21-day onboarding process in the simulated scenario and identified bottlenecks in ownership, checklist clarity, late security review, and progress visibility.
- Designed a guided onboarding flow with a 10-day pilot target, stage owners, completion criteria, and a single progress view for customers and internal teams.
- Applied Agile/Scrum across 3 two-week sprints, with backlog refinement, sprint goals, reviews, and retrospectives.
- Used Jira-style backlog and sprint tracking, Confluence-style documentation, and a dashboard-style view to track progress, blockers, and pilot feedback.
- Analysed simulated pilot feedback and redesigned two flow steps that caused drop-off, and moved the security check earlier in the process.
- Created a training handoff checklist and go-live playbook, with clear completion criteria before handoff.
- Significance: demonstrates end-to-end scope, schedule, risk, stakeholder, and data-informed decision-making for project management roles, in a form a hiring manager can open and discuss.

## How to label it on a resume

**Portfolio Project — Agile SaaS Onboarding Rollout (Self-initiated project)**
Followed by 3–4 of the bullets above, chosen for the specific job description. Use job-description keywords only where they genuinely apply to this project.
